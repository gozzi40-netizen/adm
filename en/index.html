The company

A.d.M. S.r.l.: biomedical technology in Concordia sulla Secchia

Based in Concordia sulla Secchia, Modena, A.d.M. S.r.l. operates in the biomedical sector. Alongside its single-use medical devices and ISIMIX system, the company is pursuing research into low-temperature sterilisation.

Discover ISIMIX for hospital pharmacies, explore our four single-use product lines or learn about our co-funded projects. For product and collaboration enquiries, contact ADM.

Questions about ADM and its products

What does ADM S.r.l. offer?

ADM presents the ISIMIX chemotherapy preparation system, four lines of single-use medical devices and the PLASMA-Ster project dedicated to cold plasma sterilisation. Meet the company.

What is ISIMIX?

ISIMIX is a system of dosing modules for chemotherapy preparation in hospital pharmacies. Multiple modules enable different therapies to be prepared in parallel. To discuss compatibility with your facility's equipment, email ADM.

What single-use product lines are available?

EOS CHEMO includes connecting sets, infusion sets and spikes for oncology. EIR LINE includes extension lines, stopcocks, caps and valves. DIKE LINE covers infusion sets and KHORS LINE covers transfusion sets.

How can I request information about a product line?

Open the product details and select the enquiry button: your email app will open with a prepared subject and message. You can also email info@admsrl.eu, specifying the product line, your organisation and the information you need.

Where is ADM S.r.l. located?

The company is located at Via Brodolini, units 2/4/6 – 41033 Concordia sulla Secchia (MO), Italy. View address and contact details.

Oncology line
Oncology line

EOS CHEMO

Connecting sets, infusion sets and accessories for the preparation and administration of antineoplastic drugs.

Infusion sets

One-, two- or four-way configurations, transparent or amber, with valved branches and needleless access. DEHP-free PVC tubing, 180 cm length and 3 × 4.1 mm diameter. Latex-free sets.

Connecting sets

Connect a bag or vial to the main EOS CHEMO line. Available in 40 and 50 cm lengths, including amber versions, with options for a 0.2 µm filter, flow regulator and valved connections.

Preparation accessories

Spikes with attachment for 20 mm vials and a 0.2 µm air vent; male needleless male-to-female Luer Lock connector with an irreversible locking mechanism. Latex-free accessories.

Extension lines and accessories
Extension lines and accessories

EIR LINE

Infusion extension lines, three-way stopcocks and closure caps. The range includes various materials, diameters, lengths and configurations, with latex-free products.

Infusion extension lines

Straight versions from 50 to 200 cm in DEHP-free PVC, PVC-free material or PE, depending on the code. Amber PE versions and coiled extension lines from 100 to 400 cm are available, as well as a version with a flow regulator.

Filters, stopcocks and caps

40 cm extension lines with 0.2 or 1.2 µm filters; three-way stopcocks with 360° rotation, also with extension lines from 10 to 150 cm. ABS caps: transparent pierceable, white male and red Combi.
